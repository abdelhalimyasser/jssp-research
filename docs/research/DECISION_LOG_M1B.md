## 1. Status Taxonomy

- **`ACCEPTED`** — Grounded in primary literature or mathematical proof and sufficiently specified to implement directly.
- **`PROVISIONAL`** — Mathematically well-defined baseline recommended for M2–M6 implementation, subject to empirical validation/calibration during Stage 1/2 (pilot/screening) before confirmatory lock.
- **`DEFERRED`** — Investigated with verified primary sources, but excluded from the frozen primary configuration matrix pending dedicated supplementary study or model extension.
- **`REJECTED`** — Investigated and formally excluded, with mathematical or methodological justification documented.

## 3. Detailed Decision Records

### `D01` — OBR Representation, Decoder, and Adapter Semantics
- **`D01-A` (`ACCEPTED`):** Serial SGS with earliest feasible machine idle-gap insertion generates **active schedules** (Giffler & Thompson 1960; Sprecher et al. 1995), strictly dominating semi-active schedules without omitting global optima as non-delay schedules can. Complete formulation, pseudocode, and worked $3 \times 3$ example are frozen in [`docs/algorithms/obr_decoder.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/algorithms/obr_decoder.md).
- **`D01-B` (`PROVISIONAL`):** Topological re-encoding via `(r_o, r_o + p_o, job_id)` preserves exact active schedules on round-trip. Moves producing semi-active non-active schedules receive an active left-shift under Serial SGS; this can only improve or preserve makespan ($C_{\max}' \le C_{\max}^{\text{graph}}$), and will be empirically instrumented in M2/M5 via `adapter_left_shift_count` and machine-order preservation assertions.

### `D02` — QMC Mapping, MWR, and Deterministic Initialization
- **`D02-A` & `D02-B` (`ACCEPTED`):** Dynamic remaining work $W_j(t)$ avoids stale initial job weights. Ascending-`JobId` ordering + half-open $[F_r, F_{r+1})$ inverse-CDF mapping produces valid OBRs without hidden bias. Sobol sequences use Joe & Kuo (2008) direction numbers up to $d_{\max} = 21,201$ with Matoušek (1998) affine scrambling on contiguous prefixes ($i = 0 \dots N_S - 1$ scrambled; $i = 1 \dots 64$ unscrambled). Sobol sizes $N_S \in \{64, 32, 16\}$ are powers of two, whereas `A13`'s $48$-point Sobol subset is explicitly labelled an empirical mixed-QMC configuration without standard dyadic digital-net balance guarantees. Instances exceeding $L = 21,201$ (Track 2a large strata / Track 2b $1000\times 1000$) are excluded from standard Sobol claims. Full specifications appear in [`docs/algorithms/qmc_mapping.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/algorithms/qmc_mapping.md).

### `D03` — N5–N8, NNS, and Batch Approximate N7
- **`D03-A` (`ACCEPTED`):** N5, N6, N7, and N8 map exclusively to Nowicki & Smutnicki (1996), Balas & Vazacopoulos (1998), Zhang et al. (2007), and Xie et al. (2023), respectively, disambiguating Kuhpfahl & Bierwirth (2016) TWT terminology.
- **`D03-B` (`DEFERRED`):** Gui et al. (2023) `NNS` offers exact necessary-and-sufficient feasibility ($u \not\leadsto JP[v]$ and $JS[u] \not\leadsto v$). Its objective description is **internally inconsistent**: Section 2 states *"minimize the total completion time"*, while Section 1 and Section 2's critical-path definition specify makespan, and Section 6.2 reports makespan `MRE` against makespan bounds. Structural feasibility propositions (Props. 7–9), the `NNS` neighborhood definition, and the empirical makespan experiments are explicitly separated. Excluded from the primary matrix to avoid premature expansion, `NNS` is scheduled as a supplementary Tabu Search ablation (`B15-NNS` vs. verified `B15 = Tabu Search + N7`) using paired initial solutions, identical evaluation budgets, and identical seeds.
- **`D03-C` (`REJECTED` for primary exact evaluation):** Ba, Liu & Yuan (2026) propose a batch approximate evaluation heuristic using slack-time recurrence across critical blocks. It is an evaluator optimization rather than a neighborhood or metaheuristic, and exact $C_{\max}$ remains authoritative for all Phase B/C comparisons.

### `D04` — Metropolis–Hastings vs. Simulated Annealing
- **`D04-A` (`REJECTED` — prunes `B25`–`B28`):** Analysis in [`docs/algorithms/mh_vs_sa.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/algorithms/mh_vs_sa.md) distinguishes zero reverse proposal probability ($q_k(\sigma \mid \sigma') = 0$), rejection of a single transition ($\alpha_{\text{MH}} = 0$), mutually supported proposal graph connectivity ($G_k^{\leftrightarrow}$), global irreducibility, and Markov-chain ergodicity. Unverified claims of global non-irreducibility and exact `N6`–`N8` $\to$ `N5` collapse are formally downgraded to unverified structural hypotheses. Practical pruning of `B25`–`B28` (67 $\to$ 63 configurations) is retained because fixed-$T$ MH without Hastings correction duplicates SA (`B21`–`B24`), whereas exact Hastings correction incurs full reverse-neighborhood enumeration overhead and rejects transitions with $q_k(\sigma \mid \sigma') = 0$.
- **`D04-B` (`ACCEPTED`):** `B29`–`B32` remain active as a **Metropolis-filtered ILS perturbation followed by deterministic local search**, operating on OBR chromosome states $\pi \in \Pi^n_m$ with $E(\pi) = C_{\max}(\mathrm{Decode}(\pi))$ via the 3-cycle distinct-job OBR perturbation kernel ($D_{3\text{cycle}} = 2 \sum_{a<b<c} m_a m_b m_c$, symmetric on $\Pi^n_m$ with unique inverses and 1-to-1 successor mapping; non-symmetric on the quotient schedule space $\mathcal{S}_{\text{A}}$) with no claim of composite detailed balance.

### `D05`, `D06`, and `D07`
- **`D05` (`ACCEPTED` protocol / `PROVISIONAL` scalars):** Exact algorithms, parameter tables, and budget-exhaustion rules are frozen in [`docs/experiments/phase_b.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/experiments/phase_b.md).
- **`D06` (`PROVISIONAL`):** Model A (Pair-Level Selection Gate) is selected over Model B (Reproductive-Slot Gate) to guarantee persistent dual-lineage preservation (`C05`–`C09`) without implicit view starvation. Exact equations and novelty comparison against El-Mihoub et al. (2021) are in [`docs/algorithms/dual_view.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/algorithms/dual_view.md).
- **`D07` (`ACCEPTED`):** Classical GA baseline, 20-seed schedule, stratified dev/test splits, statistical tests, and Track 4/5 isolation are specified in [`docs/experiments/phase_c.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/experiments/phase_c.md) and [`docs/experiments/fairness_protocol.md`](file:///home/abdelhalimyasser/Downloads/jssp-research/docs/experiments/fairness_protocol.md).
